# Community standards

We aim to keep project spaces welcoming, respectful, and focused on useful collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, constructive, and respectful of different experience levels.
- Discuss technical evidence and ideas without attacking people.
- Accept good-faith corrections and give feedback that helps others improve.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing someone's personal information or hardware-test data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or security reports without consent.
- Deliberately misleading compatibility, test, licensing, or funding claims.
- Using donations, compute, or AI-generated contribution volume to pressure maintainers or claim project authority.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, or restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through a verified channel; do not publish personal or sensitive details in an issue. The owner should document an appropriate private contact route before public launch.

Project roles do not exempt anyone from these standards. Decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and focused on keeping participation safe.
